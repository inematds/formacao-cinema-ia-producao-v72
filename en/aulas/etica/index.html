Lesson 7 · 18 min

Ethics and law: label, image and voice rights, model license

Before you publish, three questions: do viewers know it was made with AI? Is everything that appears in the film yours or authorized? What does the law where you are require today? The laws are still changing, so this lesson teaches the principle and gives each rule with its source and date.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Label what was made with AI in a way the audience can see
  2. 2Know what the invisible watermark solves and what it doesn't
  3. 3Check people, voices, brands, and music before publishing
  4. 4Read the model license and keep a record of what was generated

Goal: check your film before publishing: label, people and voices, brands, music, model license, and a record of what was generated.

02 · What the law and the cases teach (Oct/2026)

Principle first, rule with source and date after.

1. Visible label

In the European Union, Article 50 of the AI Act has applied since 08/02/2026: anyone who publishes a deepfake must add a visible or audible label. The Code of Practice on marking and labeling came out on 06/10/2026 and says a machine watermark alone is not enough.

For a clearly artistic or satirical work the requirement is milder. Outside the EU, a label is good practice, and many platforms have their own rules.

2. Watermarks and credentials

SynthID (Google) is an invisible watermark; C2PA are content credentials written into the file. According to a secondary source (May/2026), Google announced verification of both in Search and Chrome.

The limit: metadata can be removed, and a watermark only covers the generators that take part. That is why the label the audience sees is still necessary.

3. People, voices and characters

Seedance 2.0 clashed with the MPA, the association of US film studios, for generating actors and characters. The Sora app, with its "cameos" of real people, shut down on 04/26/2026, and the API on 09/24/2026.

In the US, the NO FAKES Act, which protects image and voice against digital replicas, passed the Senate committee on 06/18/2026 and was stalled on the floor as of 10/07/2026.

4. Brazil and licenses

Brazil, reported by the press and not confirmed here in an official source: PL 2338/2023 (the AI framework bill) passed the Senate on 12/10/2024 and was still in the Chamber in 2026; a TSE resolution would address synthetic content in the 2026 elections.

Model license: Wan 2.2 is Apache 2.0; LTX-2.3 uses a community license, paid above US$ 10 million in revenue. Read the license before using it for work.
