02 · How to finish the short film

From the folder of files to the checked film.

1. The project folder

One folder per film, with the plan, the sound map, the color recipe, the checklist, the label, the clips and the final file. Names by shot number.

It is the folder that lets you remake the film if a tool changes its price or shuts down, as happened with Sora in 2026.

Foldera-primeira-fornada/ · plano-edicao.txt · mapa-som.txt · cor.txt · checklist.txt · rotulo.txt · clipes/ · curta.mp4

2. Assemble in order

Cut first (lesson 3), then the score and the mix (lesson 2), then color (lesson 4), and subtitles and label last. Touching the color before the final cut means doing the work twice.

No generated clips? A film made from the photos in lesson 5 works, or the rehearsal clips repeated with a title card, like the example that passes (32 s). What gets checked is the file.

3. Check and deliver

Export as MP4 H.264 and check below. If your edit plan calls for a 9:16 cut, reframe it by hand (subject in the center) and check that too: the page accepts both formats.

Watch the whole film with headphones, in one sitting, preferably the next day, before you publish.

04 · Optional: check in the terminal

Only for people who already use a terminal and have ffmpeg installed. In the course folder downloaded from GitHub; for your own file, replace --exemplo with its name (curta.mp4).

Terminal (in the course folder)
python3 conferir.py curta --exemplo; echo "saída: $?"
python3 conferir.py curta --errado; echo "saída: $?"
Example from the repository
$ python3 conferir.py curta --exemplo; echo "saída: $?"
OK: 32,0 s · 1280×720 · 16:9
saída: 0
$ python3 conferir.py curta --errado; echo "saída: $?"
Ainda não. O que falta:
 - O vídeo tem 20,0 s; o pedido é de 30 a 60 s.
 - O vídeo é 960×720 (4:3); o pedido é 16:9 / 9:16.
saída: 1

05 · Final practice

Your short film, from plan to file. Generating the shots is up to whatever tool you have; photos and rehearsal clips count too.

Short film · 30 to 60 s checked

2 h

Assemble the short film following your edit plan, with sound, color, subtitles and label, and export it as MP4 H.264. Choose the file in "Check your short film" and save it in the project folder as curta.mp4.

Done when: the page answers OK: [s] s · [w]×[h] · [p], with a duration between 30 and 60 s and a 16:9 or 9:16 format.

Deliverable · the social media version

40 min

Make the second deliverable from your plan (for example, the 9:16 cut reframed by hand) and check that too.

Done when: the page answers OK: … for both versions, each with its own format in the answer.

Folder · the complete project

15 min

Check that the project folder has the plan, the sound map, the color recipe, the checklist, the label and the short film.

Done when: the folder has plano-edicao.txt, mapa-som.txt, cor.txt, checklist.txt, rotulo.txt and curta.mp4, and each .txt passes again in the checker from its lesson.
