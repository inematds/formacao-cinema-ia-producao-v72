Lesson 2 · 18 min

Sound as a character: native dialogue, voice, score and effects

In 2026 sound stopped being the last step: the main generators return ambience, effects and speech together with the image. This forces you to decide, shot by shot, what is born with the video and what comes in later, like the score and the narration. This lesson settles that decision in a sound map.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Split the sound into layers: ambience, effect, line, score and silence
  2. 2Decide what comes out with the video and what goes in at the edit
  3. 3Choose score and voice with a clear license and your own voice
  4. 4Mix with the voice in front and the music underneath

Goal: decide, shot by shot, what is heard and what comes out with the video or separately, and build the scene's sound map.

01 · The sound layers of a shot

Five layers. Click each one and see where it usually comes from.

Click each block of the diagram to see what it does.

Rule of thumb: what comes from the action of the shot can come out with the video; what crosses shots goes into the edit.

02 · How to decide the sound, step by step

In the scene "The first batch", using the sound column of the shot list from course 0.

1. Native or separate

Native audio became the standard in 2026 in the main generators (dated examples, Oct/2026: Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.x, Runway Gen-4.5). Seedance 2.5 accepts up to 10 reference audios per generation.

Use native for what comes from the shot: ambience, the effect of the action, a short line. Keep separate what crosses shots, what has to sound the same in all of them, and what will change language.

In the batchWind and knocks on the door: native. Score and opening narration: separate, in the edit.

2. Layers and silence

Diegetic sound exists in the scene and the character hears it; non-diegetic sound only the audience hears, like the score. A well-resolved shot has ambience and the effect of the action.

Silence is a choice too: cut the music before the turning point, leave one shot with only the ambience, a breath after the impact. If you can show it, don't narrate it.

3. Licensed score and voice

Music generators change fast: Suno released v5.5 on 03/26/2026; a v6 and ElevenLabs' Eleven v3 appear only as reported, not confirmed by an official source. Read each tool's license of use before you publish.

Synthetic or cloned voice: only your own, or from someone who authorized it in writing (lesson 7). Free, local route: Concat cleans and levels the voice and has an AI voice.

4. Mix with a hierarchy

The voice dominates; the music stays atmospheric, underneath; the effect appears at the moment of the action. The most common mistake is leaving the music at the same volume as the voice.

Starting point: lower the score until the line is clear without effort, then check with headphones. Leave a few extra seconds of score for the exit fade.

03 · Templates to copy

The format the page checks, and a request for the AI assistant to draft the map from the shot list.

mapa-som.txt (template)
Scene: <name> (<duration> s)
Score: <style, where it comes in and where it goes out; with or without vocals>
# n | what you hear | origin
1 | ambience: <...>; effect: <...> | origin: <native or separate>
2 | line: "<short sentence>" | origin: <native or separate>
Request to the AI assistant (draft of the sound map)
You are a film sound editor. Read the shot list below and propose a sound map, one line per shot, in this format:
n | what you hear (ambience, effect, line, score or silence) | origin: native or separate
Rules:
- native = comes out with the generated video (ambience, effect of the action, short line);
- separate = goes in the edit (score, long narration, sound that repeats the same across several shots);
- only one origin per shot;
- leave at least one shot with only ambience before a line;
- on the first line, describe the score: style, where it comes in and where it goes out, no vocals under the line.
Shot list: <paste your shot list here>

08 · To keep

Native or separateWhat comes from the shot goes out with it; what runs across shots goes into the edit.
Silence is soundA shot with only ambience before the turning point gives it weight.
The voice up frontMusic underneath, a spot effect, a check with headphones. Cloned voice: only yours or an authorized one.
